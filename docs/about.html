About Elsewhere

We research weekends the way most people research a decade-long move.

Where It Starts

Weekends shouldn't begin with searching

The workweek is almost over. Two free days are ahead, and all you want is a change of scenery — to explore somewhere new, take a deep breath in a different place, and come home on Sunday evening feeling that your weekend was truly well spent.

Instead, the search begins. Google Maps, saved Instagram posts, friends' recommendations, blogs, official websites, conflicting reviews. An hour later, you have dozens of tabs open and still no clear answer.

That's why this project exists: to make choosing a great weekend as enjoyable as living it.

The Work You Don't See

Every article is backed by far more than you read

An article may take only a few minutes to read. Researching it can take days. We study maps, official sources, local websites and blogs, reviews in different languages, photos, videos, seasonal conditions, transportation and countless small details.

Not because we want to write longer articles — because we want to answer one simple question: is this place truly worth one of your precious weekends?

Our Standard

"A beautiful photograph is often enough to spark our curiosity. It is never enough to publish an article. If, after all our research, we wouldn't choose to spend our own weekend there, you won't find it on this website."

Honesty First

We're not trying to convince you to go

This project isn't about persuading you to travel at any cost. If a waterfall dries up in summer, if the drive takes longer than people claim, if the famous bloom lasts only a few weeks, or if the iconic view exists only from a drone — we'd rather tell you before your trip than let you discover it the hard way.

To us, a successful article isn't one that makes someone book a ticket. It's one that makes them come home thinking, "It was exactly as described."

Is This For You?

This project might be for you if…

01

You've been living in a new city for months rather than days, and haven't stopped to explore it properly.

02

Work fills most of your week, but you don't want to postpone discovering your surroundings until your next vacation.

03

You're drawn to places beyond the usual tourist routes, and you'd rather come home with meaningful memories than another collection of photos.

We can't give you more weekends. But we can do everything we can to help you spend less time searching for them — and more time living them.
