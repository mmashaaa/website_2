How to Visit the La Serenísima Factory

3 min read
Cultural Experience
Home/ Argentina/ How to Visit the La Serenísima Factory
On This Trip

From the milking parlour to the supermarket shelf — a look inside Argentina's biggest dairy.

Most factories remain hidden from us. We see only the finished product, never the long chain of people, technology, and machinery that makes it possible.

A tour of the La Serenísima factory offers a rare chance to step behind those closed doors and discover how one of Argentina’s largest dairy producers operates. This is not a museum filled with displays or an interactive attraction. Instead, visitors walk along elevated viewing galleries overlooking the active production floor, watching everyday dairy products make their journey from raw milk to finished packaging.

See the Factory in Action

During the tour, visitors walk along specially designed observation galleries suspended above the production areas. From here, you can watch the key stages of milk processing, automated production lines, packaging, and the palletizing of finished products.

It is a rare opportunity to look inside a working food-processing facility—one that is normally closed to the public. The tour offers a new appreciation for the countless steps involved in producing something as ordinary as the carton of milk or yogurt you see every day in the supermarket.

Good to Know
📍Location: La Serenísima Factory, General Rodríguez.
Good to Know
⏳Duration: Approximately 1.5 hours.

Visit the Company’s Museum

After the factory tour, the experience continues in the La Serenísima Museum, a small exhibition dedicated to the company’s history.

Here, visitors learn how one of Argentina’s best-known dairy brands was founded, how its production expanded over the decades, and how dairy technology has evolved. The museum turns the visit into more than just a factory tour—it also offers a glimpse into the history of Argentina’s dairy industry.

Things to Know Before You Visit

Tours are available by advance reservation only. The company welcomes families, school groups, and other organized visits.

“Tours are available by advance reservation only. The company welcomes families, school groups, and other organized visits.”

Photography and video recording are not permitted inside the production areas. Comfortable closed-toe shoes are recommended, as the route includes staircases and a considerable amount of walking.

At the end of the visit, guests are offered a light snack.

Who Will Enjoy This Tour?

A visit to the La Serenísima factory is unlikely to be the main reason to travel to General Rodríguez. However, if you enjoy industrial tourism or have ever wondered how modern food production works, it makes an unusual and rewarding addition to your trip.

After seeing the process firsthand, an ordinary carton of milk on a supermarket shelf feels a little different. Instead of seeing only the finished product, you’ll remember the complex world of people, technology, and precision that usually remains hidden behind factory doors.
