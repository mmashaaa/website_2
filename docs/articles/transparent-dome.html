Spend the Night Under a Transparent Dome Near Buenos Aires

3 min read
Slow Down in NatureOne-Night Escape
Home/ Argentina/ Spend the Night Under a Transparent Dome Near Buenos Aires
On This Trip

A glass geodesic dome under the open pampas sky turns one night into the whole trip.

Sometimes the best escape doesn’t begin with a new city or a packed itinerary. Sometimes it starts with one simple decision—to spend a night somewhere that feels completely different from your everyday routine.

Just outside Carlos Keen lies Don Aniceto, a countryside retreat where people come not only for its transparent domes, but for the feeling of trading a city apartment, if only for one night, for trees, silence, and a morning without an alarm clock.

Here, you don’t have to choose between nature and comfort. You can spend the day by the pool, enjoy an unhurried dinner, sleep surrounded by greenery, and wake up with sunlight streaming through transparent walls instead of the sound of your phone.

Spend a Summer Day by the Pool

During the warmer months, a countryside getaway near Carlos Keen offers much more than village walks and long lunches. Several rural properties feature swimming pools, sun loungers, and spacious gardens where you can spend a hot afternoon much like you would at a boutique countryside resort.

Experiencia Keen offers a swimming pool, while photos shared by Don Aniceto also show guests relaxing by the water, enjoying picnics, and spending romantic weekends together. It creates an entirely different rhythm for the day: explore the surrounding area in the morning, spend a few peaceful hours by the pool after lunch, and never feel the need to rush home.

It is an ideal option for anyone looking to escape Buenos Aires without spending the whole weekend on the road.

Good to Know
☀️Best time to visit: During the warmer months. Be sure to check the pool’s opening schedule in advance.
Good to Know
🎒What to bring: A swimsuit, towel, sunscreen, and a change of clothes. It is also worth checking whether towels and sun loungers are provided.

Spend the Night in a Transparent Dome

Perhaps the most unusual way to spend a weekend near Carlos Keen is to stay in one of Don Aniceto’s transparent domes.

“Perhaps the most unusual way to spend a weekend near Carlos Keen is to stay in one of Don Aniceto’s transparent domes.”

These geodesic domes stand on wooden platforms surrounded by trees. They are neither tents nor ordinary hotel rooms. Inside, you’ll find a comfortable bed and everything needed for a relaxing overnight stay, while the transparent walls create the feeling of sleeping almost beneath the open sky.

It is an especially appealing choice for a romantic getaway. Spend the day by the pool, enjoy dinner outdoors, then fall asleep surrounded by nature. The next morning, instead of opening your eyes to another hotel building, you’ll wake up to trees and sunlight all around you.

Carlos Keen itself can easily be explored in a single day, but spending the night here changes the entire rhythm of the trip. What begins as a short countryside excursion becomes a genuine break between two busy workweeks.

Good to Know
📍Where to stay: Don Aniceto.
Good to Know
🌙Best for: Couples and anyone looking for an unusual overnight stay within easy reach of Buenos Aires.
Good to Know
📅Before booking: Check what is included in the rate, whether the dome has a private bathroom, heating or air conditioning, meals, and access to the swimming pool.

Wake Up Surrounded by Trees

The most memorable part of the experience is not the transparent dome itself, but the morning that follows.

There is no need to rush, pack your bags, or head straight back to the city. Instead, you can enjoy a slow breakfast, step outside with a cup of coffee, wander through the grounds, or simply sit quietly for a few minutes and appreciate the silence.

If you are already planning a visit to Carlos Keen, spending the night here easily transforms a day trip into a proper weekend escape. And if you were not planning to visit at all, this unusual stay might just become the reason to discover this peaceful corner of the Argentine countryside.
