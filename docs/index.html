The Idea

Choosing a great weekend should be as enjoyable as living it.

An article here takes minutes to read. Researching it takes days — maps, official sources, local blogs, seasonal conditions, transport, the small details that decide whether a place is actually worth one of your weekends.

If, after all that, we wouldn't spend our own weekend there, you won't find it on this site.
