Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Elsewhere ("we," "us," "this site") publishes travel journal content about weekend trips in Argentina, Thailand, and Serbia. This page explains what information is collected from visitors, how it's used, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

We do not require an account or ask you to submit personal information to read this site. The information we do receive falls into two categories:

  • Analytics data. We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site — pages viewed, approximate location (city/country level), device and browser type, and how you arrived here. This is collected automatically through cookies and similar technologies.
  • Information you provide directly. If a contact form, newsletter signup, or comment feature is added to the site in the future, we will only collect what's needed to respond to you or deliver what you signed up for.

Cookies

This site uses cookies set by Google Analytics to distinguish visitors and measure site usage. You can block or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings, or opt out of Google Analytics tracking using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. Blocking cookies will not affect your ability to read articles on this site.

How We Use Information

Analytics data is used only to understand which destinations and stories readers find useful, and to improve the site. We do not sell visitor data, and we do not share it with third parties except the analytics provider named above, under their own privacy terms.

Third-Party Links

Articles on this site may link out to official tourist information, local businesses, or transport providers mentioned in a story. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those external websites — please review their own policies before sharing information with them.

Data Retention & Your Rights

Analytics data is retained according to Google Analytics' standard retention settings. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information collected about you. To make such a request, contact us using the details below.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time as the site evolves. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent through the contact details listed in the site footer.
