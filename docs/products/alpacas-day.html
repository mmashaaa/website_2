A Day Among Alpacas and More

5 min read
Slow Down in NatureAnimals & Wildlife
Home/ Argentina/ A Day Among Alpacas and More
On This Trip

An unhurried farm afternoon spent among alpacas, far from any checklist of famous sights.

After several days in front of a computer, sometimes all you want is something real. To run your hand through the soft wool of an alpaca, pet a curious calf, wander across the grass without rushing, and forget about the city for a few hours.

That is exactly why people come to Experiencia Keen, just outside the village of Carlos Keen. Here you can spend nearly an entire day surrounded by animals, enjoy a leisurely lunch, relax by the pool, and discover that sometimes the best way to recharge is simply to spend time in nature.

For many visitors, Experiencia Keen alone is reason enough to make the trip to Carlos Keen. This modern countryside retreat combines a working farm, spacious green grounds, and places to unwind. There is no need to keep wondering what to do next. You can simply stroll, observe the animals, relax, and allow yourself to enjoy an unhurried day.

Meet the Alpacas

The alpacas have become the unmistakable symbol of Experiencia Keen. They appear in almost every photo shared by the property, and they are usually the first stop for visitors.

Yet it is not only the animals themselves that leave such an impression. For many people, this is a rare opportunity to watch them peacefully, come close, feel their remarkably soft fleece, and spend as much time with them as they like. Moments like these are difficult to find in everyday city life, which is why they often become the most memorable part of the visit.

Good to Know
📷A tip: The alpacas may be the most photogenic residents of the farm, but don’t reach for your phone immediately. Sometimes a few quiet minutes spent beside them become a far stronger memory than any photograph.

Spend the Day Among Animals

The experience does not end with the alpacas. Sheep, calves, goats, poultry, and other farm animals also live on the property. Depending on the day’s program, visitors may have the chance to feed some of them or get up close.

The animals are not a separate attraction—they are simply part of a wide, green landscape. Between walks, you can rest beneath the trees, sit in the grass, or simply watch the slow rhythm of country life unfold around you. That is why families with children, couples, and adults looking to escape the pace of the city all come here for the day.

Experience an Authentic Argentine

Día de Campo

At Experiencia Keen, you are not simply visiting a farm—you can enjoy a genuine día de campo, the traditional Argentine countryside experience that locals consider a destination in itself.

The exact program depends on the property, but it usually includes several meals, walks through the grounds, time to relax outdoors, and opportunities to interact with the animals. Some packages also include horseback riding, rides in a traditional two-wheeled sulky cart, access to the swimming pool, or seasonal activities.

The point of a día de campo is not to pack the day with entertainment. People arrive in the morning and stay until evening without thinking about where to go next. While children explore the farm or play outdoors, adults can enjoy a leisurely lunch and appreciate the rare luxury of having nothing to plan.

Good to Know
📅Before you go: Check exactly what is included in your chosen día de campo. Depending on the venue, it may range from a simple country lunch to a full-day experience with animals, a swimming pool, and additional activities.
Good to Know
⏳How much time to allow: Plan to spend almost the entire day.

Spend a Summer Day by the Pool

During the warmer months, Experiencia Keen offers much more than time with animals. The property features a swimming pool, sun loungers, and spacious green areas where you can spend a hot afternoon much like you would at a small countryside resort.

It completely changes the rhythm of the day. You might begin by meeting the alpacas and other animals, enjoy a relaxed lunch, and then spend several peaceful hours beside the water. It is an ideal option for anyone who wants to escape Buenos Aires without spending the whole weekend traveling.

Good to Know
☀️Best time to visit: During the warm season. Be sure to check pool opening dates and hours in advance.
Good to Know
🎒What to bring: A swimsuit, towel, sunscreen, and a change of clothes. It is also worth checking whether towels and sun loungers are provided on-site.

What Not to Expect

Experiencia Keen is best enjoyed not as a place where you try to fit in as many activities as possible, but as somewhere to slow down and spend a peaceful day in nature. The goal is not to move constantly from one attraction to another, but to enjoy the feeling created by the animals, quiet walks, a long lunch, and a few hours away from the city.

Before your visit, it is also worth checking the current schedule, whether animal feeding is available, and what activities are offered that day. Some experiences vary depending on the season.

If One Day Isn’t Enough

Many visitors arrive planning to spend only a day at Experiencia Keen, but by evening they realize they are not ready to head back to the city. You may find yourself feeling the same way.

Fortunately, near Carlos Keen there is an unusual place where you can stay overnight and extend your countryside escape for another day. We’ll tell you all about it in the next article.

Some places are worth visiting once simply to tick them off your list. Others leave you planning your next visit before you’ve even made it home. Experiencia Keen feels very much like the latter.
